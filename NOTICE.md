# Notice

The files in `text/` and `glossary.tsv` are English translations of lines of Swords of Legends Online's text, written for Fenji. Each line is keyed by a fingerprint of the game's own line. They hold none of the game's text in Chinese, none of its own English, and none of its files.

The game, its text and its names belong to their rights holders. These translations are made for players of their own legitimately obtained copies of the game, and Fenji's launcher applies them only to such a copy. They aren't sold, and they aren't a substitute for the game.

If you hold rights in the game and want something here changed or removed, open an issue, or reach the maintainers privately through the contact in [Fenji's security policy](https://github.com/theintonerzero/fenji/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
